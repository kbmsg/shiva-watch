Bereavement Notices הודעות שכול 
 We regret to announce the passing of Bracha Susswein z"l, the sister of our member Shalom Bronner.   
As there are family members coming from the States, the time of the funeral has not yet been announced. Shalom will be sitting shiva at his sister's home in Modi'in, 49/23 Yigal Yadin. 
Further details will be shared when they become available. 

 May Hashem comfort Shalom among the other mourners of Zion and Jerusalem.
