Bereavement Notices הודעות שכול 
 We regret to announce the passing of Bracha Susswein z"l , the sister of our member Shalom Bronner .   
The funeral is scheduled for  Monday, November 2, at the Modi'in cemetery, at 11:00 . 
 Shalom will be sitting shiva at his sister's home in Modi'in, 49/23 Yigal Yadin through Wednesday night. 
 He will return to Rehovot on Thursday and will sit from Thursday morning (no Shacharit service), Friday, Saturday night, and Sunday morning. 
 Davening times are Shacharit: 6:15, Mincha and Ma'ariv: from 16:30. 
 Visiting hours:  from 8:00 - 21:00. The address is Ephraim Katzir 4, Apt. 7.
